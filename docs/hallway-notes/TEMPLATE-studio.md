## 3 · The outcomes

| Outcome | yes / no | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Stated what the candidate does, correctly, inside Part 1's 90s** | | their answer, verbatim |
| **2. Grabbed the wheel unprompted** (any canvas interaction, Part 2) | | timestamp — or `no` |
| **3. Reached the keep rail** (export or share touched, Part 2) | | timestamp — or `no` |
| **4. Could say what the replay was** (a recorded agent run, replayed — their words) | | their answer to "what just happened" |

Outcome 1: judge against what the site actually claims — "something with AI and design" is a
**no**. Outcomes 2–3: `yes` only if unprompted. Outcome 4: judge whether their answer distinguishes
"a recording of something real" from "an animation" — the honesty contract's cold read.

## 4 · Where they stalled

## 5 · What they did not see

cards, the take-over, the keep rail, the provenance flip.
