Live import fidelity

Build brief for ticket #474. The full plan: import-live-fidelity-474.md

What we're building and why

Today, importing a design from Brilliant says "fidelity: missing", because nothing ever compares the picture with what we built. After this, one button renders our version, compares the colours, and answers green or red. One grey in today's neutral theme fails a contrast check, so every live answer reads red until that grey is fixed; the colour gap still tells right from wrong.

The concepts this plan rests on

Headless rendering

A browser with no window draws the page and saves a screenshot. The name is headless Chromium. We need it to turn our built component into pixels.

composition → browser → candidate.png

A separate helper process

The portal starts a small program, waits for its answer, then carries on. That is a child process (a program another program launches). The browser library stays out of the portal.

portal ──spawn──▶ measure-render
       ◀── png + boxes ──

Colour difference per part

For each text or icon, average its ink colour in both pictures and measure the gap. The number is ΔE (how different two colours look to a person). Over 5 reads red.

example numbers, not measured:
title  #1a1a1a vs #1a1a1a → ΔE 0.9  close
title  #1a1a1a vs #1a7f37 → ΔE 30   far

See-through pixels

Brilliant's export has a clear background, so edge pixels are partly see-through. We lay both pictures on the same white sheet first, then trim ours to the same size. That step is alpha flattening (alpha = how opaque a pixel is).

The plan at a glance

  1. Phase 1: the pure maths (flatten, part boxes, record rebuild). Needs nothing.
  2. Phase 2: the drawing helper. Runs in parallel with phase 1.
  3. Phase 3: route, button, "an edit clears the result". Needs 1 and 2.
  4. Phase 4: the owner draws a right and a wrong frame; tests replay them. Needs 3.
