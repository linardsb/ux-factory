Launch and criteria decisions

Build brief for ticket #517. The full plan: discovery-owner-decisions-517.md

What we're building and why

Nothing is coded. Today, no discovery question asks how a product launches, and no PRD line carries a test of "done". After one sitting, the owner has decided where each belongs, and any build is filed as its own ticket.

The two decisions

Launch: where pilots and rollback plans get asked. Criteria: whether top-priority lines carry pass/fail tests.

The concepts this plan rests on

1. Who decides, who records

The owner's words are the decision. The agent only lays out options and copies the answer exactly. This is the honesty contract (a rule that no agent writes the owner's half).

agent:  options + costs   (labelled "agent")
owner:  types the verdict
agent:  quotes it, word for word, with the date

2. Content versus filing

An owner can say something, and the agent then files it under a heading (a rung, one level of the requirements ladder). The ticket treated the agent's heading as the owner's choice. The plan separates the two, because another ticket counts that same filing as a mistake.

3. A question budget

Full discovery asks at most a fixed number of questions, like a page limit. After ticket #511 the limit is 33. Adding a question everywhere raises it to 34, past a limit the owner agreed not to raise. Adding it to one optional module fits, but only reaches runs that tick that box.

4. Negative tests already exist

Every recorded decision already carries a "wrong if" line: what would prove it false. That is a test written from the failure side. So the criteria question is whether to add the success side too.
