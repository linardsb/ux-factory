Claude contradiction screen

Build brief · the full plan: claude-contradiction-screen-466.md

What we're building and why

Today nothing reliably spots two sentences in a product spec (a PRD) that cannot both be true. The cheap classifier we tried found none of three known clashes. After this, one Claude call reads the whole spec and names the clashing pairs, and we measure it once.

The concepts this plan rests on

Pre-registration

Write down the test and the pass mark before seeing any result. Here the prompt, model and scoring rule are saved in one commit (a dated snapshot in git) before the one paid call. Otherwise a good score could come from tweaking.

commit 1: "this is the question, this is how we score it"
commit 2: the one answer, saved exactly as it came

Quote → claim mapping

Claude answers in quotes; the scoring sheet uses claim ids like c044. The mapper finds each quote in the document and names the one claim owning those lines. Two owners, or none, earns no credit.

"The bank itself. Seeded from…"  → lines 155–173 → c044
"ten stages"  (in c044 and c048)  → ambiguous → no credit

Replay (record once, check forever)

The paid answer is saved exactly as it came. The free checks then feed it back through the real code and confirm the same pairs and score. Like re-watching a match recording instead of replaying the match.

Injection seam

The screen never calls Claude itself; whoever runs it hands over an ask function. The server hands over the real call; the checks hand over a recording or a deliberate failure.
