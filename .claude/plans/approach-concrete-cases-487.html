The concepts

Start with concrete cases

Build for a few real situations first, and generalise later. Every extra "what if" multiplies the work. The paragraph names this idea, from Ryan Singer, as the reason.

v1:  one owner, one screen      → works
v2:  generalise from what worked

Citing without borrowing authority

We say the site follows his advice. We never say he approves of the site. The first is honest attribution; the second would be an endorsement nobody gave.
