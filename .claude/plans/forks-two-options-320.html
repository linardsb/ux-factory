Two options at a fork

Build brief for ticket #320. The full plan: forks-two-options-320.md

What we're building and why

Today, when the PRD (the product requirements document) leaves a decision open, the agent quietly picks an answer and writes it into a screen. After this change, it drafts two versions of that screen, you pick one, and the record keeps both.

The concepts this rests on

A fork

A spot where the plan has not decided yet. Here that means an open question nobody answered, or a decision you mark as open when you ask. Like a road sign with no arrow.

fork 11: "first-payment limit?"
  → nobody decided it

Sibling proposals

Normally the agent proposes one screen at a time. A fork allows exactly two at once: the same screen, labelled option A and option B. The server adds the labels, not the agent, so they can be trusted.

One save, two verdicts

Picking B saves "accept B" and "refuse A" together, as one atomic write (both land or neither does). It is like signing a contract that cancels the other offer in the same signature.

accepted  B  at 10:02:07
refused   A  at 10:02:07

Derived, not stored

Nothing writes the word "not-picked". The system works it out: A was refused, and its sibling was accepted. So the fork list and the "not-picked" label are worked out from the record each time, and can't drift out of step with it.

How the pieces fit

Open question or your flag→ Fork turn: agent drafts twice→ Option AOption B→ You pick B, one save→ B becomes the screen A: refused, not-picked
