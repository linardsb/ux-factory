## Deviations from the plan

1. **Neutral pins are resolved to literals, not `cssValue(node.$value)` verbatim.** Contract
   colour `$value`s are aliases (`{neutral.primitives.color-ink}`); `cssValue` renders those as
   `var(--color-ink)`, and saulera does not define the neutral primitives — the "neutral" layer
   would break or follow the pack. `concreteValue()` follows the alias chain through the raw
   source (kept on `contract.src`) to a literal. Same intent, correct under every pack.
2. **Four baselines churned, not six.** factory's `#roundtrip-diff` sits inside an evidence tab
   panel that is not the active tab at rest, so `factory-*.png` regenerated byte-identical (both
   were deleted first — this is a real measurement, not tolerance swallowing). approach ×2
   (62→65) + roundtrip ×2 committed.
3. **`.cmp-strip-cell` wraps the hex as a whole unit** (flex-wrap instead of
   `overflow-wrap:anywhere`, which sheared hexes mid-value at 360px — caught on the eyeball pass).
